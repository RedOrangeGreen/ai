Local First

Local-first apps run on your device, not in the cloud. Your data stays private and the app works offline.

They're coded by AI and designed to be:

  • Private — no accounts, no servers, no tracking.
  • Offline — works without an internet connection.
  • Instant — zero setup, just open and use.
