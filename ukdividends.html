Market context — early November 2026

The backdrop the UK yields below sit in, as reported in the week to Friday 6 November.

  • The FTSE 100 is still up year to date but slipped in the latest week. It closed at 10,673.94 on 6 Nov, down 1.9% from the 16 Oct close of 10,881.90 and 0.5% lower on the week, as the global AI selloff spilled into London.
  • Official YTD to the 6 Nov close: FTSE 100 +7.5% price-only from the 2025 close of 9,931.40. Note the chart badge above is computed from the first January datapoint rather than the 2025 close, so it reads slightly lower.
  • The Bank of England raised Bank Rate to 4.00% on 5 November. The 8–1 vote lifted the base rate from 3.75%, and savings and mortgage rates are already repricing around the move.
  • The Autumn Budget was less painful for banks than feared. The 28 October Budget doubled the bank surcharge from 3% to 6% for 15 months from January 2027, raising about £1.5bn — a smaller hit than the sector had feared, and Barclays, NatWest and Lloyds shares rose on the day.
  • Oil has come off its war highs. Brent is about $98 and WTI about $91, down from October’s $106–109. Portfolio note: LSE names here mostly pay quarterly or semi-annually — LondonMetric, Land Securities and Imperial Brands quarterly; Legal & General, M&G, Aviva and Standard Life semi-annually.
